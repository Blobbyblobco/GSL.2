    How to Play

    Everything you need to know

    Pick 3 is the easiest lottery to play in Zimbabwe. Here's exactly how it works.

    Picking your numbers

    • Choose three numbers, each from 0 to 99.
    • Order matters. 7-42-19 is different from 19-42-7.
    • No repeats. Each of your three numbers must be different.
    • Match all three in exact order to win the jackpot.
    Example pick
    07→ 42→ 19

    Ticket prices

    Two prices, so anyone can play. Both give you a shot at the jackpot.

    Standard
    Full stake ticket
    $1.00
    Entry
    Lower stake, same numbers
    $0.50

    Prizes scale with your stake. Ask your agent or check the USSD menu for the full prize breakdown.

    At an agent
    1. Find a Gold Stake agent near you.
    2. Tell them your three numbers.
    3. Pay $0.50 or $1.00 in cash.
    4. Keep your printed ticket safe.
    By USSD
    1. Dial *123# on any phone.
    2. Choose "Play Pick 3".
    3. Enter your three numbers.
    4. Pay from your mobile money.
    On WhatsApp
    1. Save +263 77 000 0000.
    2. Send us "Play" to start.
    3. Reply with your numbers.
    4. Pay and get your ticket.

    Play responsibly

    Gold Stake Lotto is a game for adults — you must be 18 or older to play. The lottery should be fun, never a way to make money or escape difficulty. Only ever spend what you can comfortably afford to lose.

    Set your own limit. Decide what you'll spend before you play, and stick to it.

    Never chase losses. The odds are the same every draw. Take a break if it stops being fun.

    Self-exclusion is available. If you need to stop, we can block your number from playing — just ask us.

    Talk to someone. If gambling is causing harm to you or your family, please reach out for support.

    Need help or want to self-exclude? Contact us on +263 77 000 0000 or email info@goldstakelotto.com. We're here to help.
